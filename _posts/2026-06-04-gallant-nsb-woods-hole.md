Dr. Jason Gallant is at the Marine Biological Laboratory (MBL) in Woods Hole, Massachusetts, to lead the genomics component of the Electric Fish Module in the Neural Systems & Behavior (NS&B) course, June 8–13, 2026.

His hands-on lesson in single-nucleus RNA-seq (snRNA-seq) asks how the androgen 11-ketotestosterone reshapes gene expression across the electrosensory system at single-cell resolution. Students prepare hormone implants, record electric organ discharges, isolate nuclei, and work through the full analysis pipeline (quality control, normalization, integration, clustering, cell-type annotation, and differential expression) using real data from the mormyrid *Brienomyrus brachyistius*.

All of the course materials are freely available online.
