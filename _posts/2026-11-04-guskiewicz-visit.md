MSU President Kevin Guskiewicz visited the Electric Fish Lab on Monday, November 2, as part of his visit to the College of Natural Science. College of Natural Science Dean Eric Hegg joined him for the tour.

In the fish room, surrounded by racks of aquaria, the group gathered around an oscilloscope to see the electric signals our fish produce.

  include figure.html
  image="images/guskiewicz-visit/jason_pres_dean.jpg"

President Guskiewicz and Dean Hegg also met [Arely Cruz-Perez](/members/arely.html), a research technician studying the genetic basis of aflagellate sperm in mormyrids, and [Swarnima Mukherjee](/members/swarnima.html), a Ph.D. student interested in the evolution of mormyrid reproduction.
