Congratulations to Alyssa Green, PhD student in the MSU Electric Fish Lab, on receiving the Great Lakes Microscopy Society 2nd Place Poster Presentation and Fan Favorite Poster Presentation!

This recognition for her poster on entitled "Using Microscopy Methods to Study Cilia and Flagella Phenotypes in CFAP221 Mutants" is based on her dissertation work trying to understand the genomic basis of aflagellete sperm in mormyrids, and was in collaboration with lab member Arely Cruz-Perez.

We are super excited for Alyssa's honor, and can't wait to share the results of her hard work, soon!
